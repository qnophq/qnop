# SaaSHub submission kit

**Name:** qnop

**Tagline:** Self-hosted document review your team actually wants to finish

**Description:**

> Document review platform for legal, procurement and product teams:
> line-precise annotations on PDF and Word documents, threaded discussions,
> an explicit review workflow that only finalizes when every annotation is
> resolved, version-aware re-anchoring with inter-version diff, teams and
> roles, OIDC SSO, audit trail — plus streaks, achievements and reviewer
> profiles. Open-core: the AGPL-3.0 Community edition is free and complete,
> self-hosted with Docker; Enterprise add-ons and a managed cloud are on the
> roadmap.
